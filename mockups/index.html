Trip Atlas — design mockups

Same data in each one. Pick the direction you like best, or mix them (e.g. A's globe with C's photo markers).

A — Night GlobeDark, cinematic. A full-screen spinning 3D globe with frosted floating panels, a trip list on the left and a detail drawer on the right. B — Field JournalLight and editorial. A scrolling travel journal next to a fixed 2D map that follows along as you read. C — ScrapbookPlayful. Polaroid photos are the map markers, there's a timeline scrubber along the bottom, and polaroid collages and a corkboard plan board live in a bottom sheet. D — Atlas Journal (A + B fusion) ★A's globe, drawer, collage, inspiration board and CRUD, plus B's large scrolling trip cards on the left that the globe follows as you scroll. Light, dark and system themes, and a bottom-sheet layout on phones. E — D-busy (D for power users)D stress-tested with ~150 trips over ten years. Opens on highlights, folds older years into one-line summaries with a year scrubber, stacks repeat trips ("New York ×13"), and adds tag / companion / place / year filters. Zoomed out, the globe shows countries shaded by visits and clustered pins; it only draws the route you're reading. Also new: undated "Someday" ideas as posterboards with a rough, draggable itinerary and a "Set dates" step, and one trip can span several countries. A dev toggle in the options menu (top right) switches to a 6-trip light user, who gets plain D.
